# Continuous Geology And Scanner

TerrainRenderer samples the existing painted atlas in world coordinates. Two material families and two different spatial scales are blended using stable FastNoiseLite fields and depth. All shared cell-edge vertices sample the same material coordinates. Noise-warped mirrored sampling, padded atlas regions, and per-vertex blending reduce visible periodicity without changing the metre grid or lighting. Static geometry, UVs, blend weights and pocket masks are cached per world; four cross-cell layer batches retain the original triangles. Lighting remains live and is memoized per vertex only within a frame. Cache invalidation covers atlas/seed, visible solid-cell topology, site identity/layout/gas flag and released pockets. This deliberately avoids setting a shader on the world CanvasItem, which also draws the ship and tools. See [performance evidence](terrain-performance.md).

Host rock appearance does not depend on buried copper/iron/crystal identity. Preventive ore icons and anchor marks are absent in normal play. The previous renderer remains an editor A/B option; it no longer draws material-specific marks, because all scanner identity belongs to the shared scanner overlay.

Exposed edges now use deterministic jagged profiles that protrude 1-5 pixels beyond the logical cell. They are drawn in a second pass after air backgrounds, so later cells cannot erase the silhouette. Adjacent wall segments share endpoint displacements. These strips are cosmetic only: no collider, mining, placement, fluid volume or navigation shape is changed. The diagnostic grid remains on top of the decorated terrain.

ScannerPulses owns a bounded transient reveal dictionary. Each expanding front records relevant cells whose centres it reaches, respecting the tool's current range. Centered 28 px icons fit inside the 40 px cells. Currently identified contents also gain a thin cell outline in their identity color; unknown signals retain only the gray icon. There are no cell fills or floating readouts. Borders and icons share the same fading strength and current identification gates. Traces have a level-dependent lifetime and are cleared on leaving scan mode, locked controls and world restore. The last part of an expiring wave is processed before it is removed, so a long frame cannot skip the outer edge. Scanning is passive: clicking/F does not perform target analysis, spend firing energy or emit an extraction beam. Tool switches clear stale beams; bomb placement and jetpack thrust remain their own actions.

Each currently identifiable cell displays its catalog icon. Detection ranges at levels I-IV remain 4/5/6/7 m, while identification ranges are 2.5/4/5.5/7 m, capped by detection. Cobalt additionally requires level III. Outside identification range or level, every material/anomaly uses the same genuinely grayscale generic scanner image, without identity colours, names or tool hints. Hover names use the existing bottom HUD and `aimed_identity`, requiring a current reveal and current player-to-cell distance; historical discoveries never bypass these gates. No group searches, HUD obstacle collection or floating label layout runs each frame.

The persistent `scan_findings` array stores cell and material/anomaly IDs. DigSite validates IDs, integral bounded coordinates, duplicate cells and the world-size limit for both direct and nested campaign restores. The field is optional for older component saves. Previously analysed deposits remain visually hidden when not scanning, including after reload; mined findings remain in the analysis history.

Sealed water cells are no longer camouflaged: the normal fluid renderer shows continuous blue water and small rising bubbles inside the arbitrary cavity mask, under existing illumination. Low-volume films have no bubbles, and all bubble geometry stays inside the current liquid rectangle. Other sealed pocket cells remain covered by host terrain and omitted from the fluid renderer until breached. Neither rendering nor scanning changes the rock seal or fluid volumes. Scanner anomalies are overlays, not leaked fluid or extra light. No new passive audio cues are included yet; Robbie's existing warnings remain active.

Natural gas pockets and their cavities are currently disabled through `hazard_layout.gd:gas_pockets_enabled`, default false. Only isolated gas-specific QA opts in to exercise retained future mechanics. Water/oil/acid generation is unchanged. New ordinary games are gas-free; older gas-containing saves are not migrated.

Tests: `test_geological_scan.gd`, `geology_scanner_smoke.gd`, updated `scanner_pulses_smoke.gd`. The native geology test compares the terrain pixels before discovery and after switching back from scanning, verifies no persistent symbol/color leak, and captures 720p/1080p output. Physics, saves, hazards, reservoirs and real tutorial regression suites remain applicable.
